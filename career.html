Analyst / Associate

Job Category : Merchant & Investment Banking

Full Time Mumbai / WFH Posted 2 days ago

Job Purpose

We are looking for a driven Analyst / Associate to join our Merchant & Investment Banking team. You will work closely with senior bankers across the deal lifecycle — from origination through execution — supporting IPOs, M&A mandates, and other capital markets transactions.

Key Responsibility

  • Build and maintain detailed financial models for IPOs, M&A transactions, and fundraising mandates.
  • Prepare pitch books, information memoranda, and other client-facing deal documents.
  • Conduct industry research and company due diligence to support transaction execution.
  • Assist in drafting prospectuses, offer documents, and regulatory filings for public issues.
  • Coordinate with legal counsel, auditors, and regulators throughout the deal process.
  • Support valuation exercises using DCF, comparable companies, and precedent transaction methods.
  • Track deal timelines and ensure all documentation is filed within regulatory deadlines.
  • Participate in client meetings and management presentations alongside senior team members.
  • Monitor market trends, peer transactions, and sector developments relevant to live mandates.
  • Assist with the preparation of board and committee presentations for client engagements.
  • Liaise with internal compliance and risk teams to ensure adherence to SEBI guidelines.
  • Contribute to the preparation of teasers and non-disclosure agreements for prospective deals.
  • Maintain accurate deal trackers and internal reporting for ongoing mandates.
  • Support the onboarding and training of incoming analysts as the team grows.

Required Experience

  • 0–3 years of experience in investment banking, equity capital markets, or a related field.
  • Strong understanding of financial statements, valuation techniques, and capital markets instruments.
  • Proficiency in Excel, PowerPoint, and financial modelling tools.
  • Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Economics, Commerce, or a related discipline; MBA or CA preferred.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Ability to work under tight deadlines across multiple live transactions.
  • High attention to detail and strong analytical thinking.
  • Familiarity with SEBI regulations and Indian capital markets is an advantage.
  • A collaborative mindset and willingness to learn in a fast-paced environment.
