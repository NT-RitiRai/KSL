IPV Startup Investment Platform

KSL has entered into a strategic Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with Inflection Point Ventures (IPV), one of India’s leading angel investing platforms, to provide our HNI clients with curated access to high-potential startup investment opportunities. Through this association, KSL clients can participate in professionally screened early-stage and growth-stage ventures across sectors such as FinTech, Consumer Tech, HealthTech, SaaS, EV, Logistics, and Digital Platforms.

Through this partnership, KSL aims to help sophisticated investors diversify beyond traditional asset classes and gain exposure to India’s innovation-led growth story. The offering is designed for HNIs seeking access to the startup ecosystem through a structured, research-driven, and professionally managed investment platform.
