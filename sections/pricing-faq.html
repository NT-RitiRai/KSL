Charges Explained

Understanding the finer details of market taxes and fees.

Tax by the government when transacting on the exchanges. Charged as above on both buy and sell sides when trading equity delivery. Charged only on selling side when trading intraday or on F&O.

When trading at KSL, STT/CTT can be a lot more than the brokerage we charge. Important to keep a tab.

A regulatory fee levied by SEBI on all transactions in the securities market, collected by the exchange and passed through to investors.

Charged by the depository (CDSL/NSDL) via KSL for debiting shares from your demat account when you sell holdings.

Levied by the stock exchanges (NSE/BSE) as a percentage of your trade turnover, charged on both buy and sell sides.

Applicable when you pledge shares from your demat account as collateral for margin trading facilities.

Periodic charges for maintaining your demat account, as applicable per the depository’s fee schedule.

Charged when placing orders related to corporate actions such as buybacks, open offers, or rights issues.

Applicable when you place an order through our dealing desk over the phone instead of the trading platform.

Charged when shares are transferred between demat accounts outside of the stock exchange.

Charged for a physical copy of your Client Master Report, sent by post on request.

Applicable when funds are added to your trading account through certain online payment gateway options.

Interest charged on outstanding dues not settled within the stipulated payment period.

A state government levy charged on the value of securities transacted, applicable on the buy side.

Additional charges or interest may apply if your trading account carries a negative (debit) balance.

Goods and Services Tax is levied at the applicable rate on brokerage and other taxable charges.
